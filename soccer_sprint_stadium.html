Welcome to the stadium

Play on a detailed perspective pitch with touchlines, penalty areas, goals, crowd stands, stadium lights, players, and shadows. Score 3 goals to level up, or switch to defending to win the ball back.

Move: WASD / arrow keys   Space: shoot / tackle   E: switch attack and defend
In DEFEND mode, press Space near the opponent with the ball to tackle. Keep them away from your goal.
